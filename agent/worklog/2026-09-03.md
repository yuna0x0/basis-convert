## A raw img tag loses the baseUrl

`window-scanned.webp` is portrait, 1438x1906, and ran about 1000px tall at column width. Capping
it with a raw `<img src="/img/window-scanned.webp" width="480" />` broke it: markdown images go
through Docusaurus's asset loader and come out `/watari-basis/assets/images/<name>-<hash>.webp`,
a raw tag keeps the path as written, and the site is served under `/watari-basis/`. It 404d on
the published site while the local build looked correct, because the check was that the tag
survived the build rather than that the path resolved.

It is now a markdown image inside `<div className="screenshot-tall">`, capped in `custom.css` at
`width: 100%; max-width: 480px`. Both are needed: `max-width` alone is more specific than
Infima's `img { max-width: 100% }` and overflows a phone. Recorded in
`docs/static/img/PLACEHOLDERS.md`.

The file was also re-encoded to 960 wide during this, and put back. Screenshots are capped in
the page, not in the file.
