## A raw img tag loses the baseUrl

`window-scanned.webp` is portrait, 1438x1906, and ran about 1000px tall at column width. Capping
it with a raw `<img src="/img/window-scanned.webp" width="480" />` broke it: markdown images go
through Docusaurus's asset loader and come out `/watari-basis/assets/images/<name>-<hash>.webp`,
a raw tag keeps the path as written, and the site is served under `/watari-basis/`. It 404d on
the published site while the local build looked correct, because the check was that the tag
survived the build rather than that the path resolved.

It is now a markdown image inside `<div className="screenshot-tall">`, capped in `custom.css` at
`width: 100%; max-width: 480px`. Both are needed: `max-width` alone is more specific than
Infima's `img { max-width: 100% }` and overflows a phone. Recorded in
`docs/static/img/PLACEHOLDERS.md`.

The file was also re-encoded to 960 wide during this, and put back. Screenshots are capped in
the page, not in the file.

## Every clipped label, not the four that were reported

`EditorGUILayout.LabelField` sizes its rect to one line whatever style it is handed, so
`EditorStyles.wordWrappedLabel` on it does nothing. Yesterday four fields were fixed because
those four were pointed at; ten other calls had the same fault and came back as a bug report on
the line under the conversion result.

All of them now go through `WrappedLabel`, which spaces the indent by hand and hands the text to
`GUILayout.Label`, since `GUILayout` ignores `EditorGUI.indentLevel`. The sites: the window
subtitle, both notes under What to convert, the prefabs summary, the rig section and each of its
messages, both tuning paragraphs, each diagnostic code and its example, what a toggle does at the
end of its row, the line under the buttons, and all four lines of the result. The result headline
also needed `wordWrap` on its own style.

Rows keep their fixed-width trailing counts on `LabelField`: they are one line by design.
